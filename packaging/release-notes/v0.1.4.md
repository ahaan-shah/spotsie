Spotify stopped accepting new sign-ins through the shared app Spotsie borrowed from other players, so Spotsie now signs in through a free Spotify app of your own, and a new onboarding walks you through making one in about two minutes. This release also keeps the player bar tidy in small and tiled windows, lets you add a whole playlist to the queue, and fixes a flicker in Repeat.

## New

- **A new onboarding.** Spotsie welcomes you with its spinning record, then shows the four steps for making your own Spotify app: open the developer dashboard, create an app, add the redirect URI (with a copy button), and tick Web API. Paste the app's Client ID and sign in. Spotsie checks the Client ID as you type, asks Spotify whether it knows it before your browser opens, and explains in plain words when Spotify turns a sign-in down. When you come back, it opens at **Welcome back** with your app filled in.
- **Add a playlist to the queue.** Playlists, including your own, now have an **Add to queue** button beside Play and in their menus. Spotsie loads any songs not yet shown, then queues them all in the playlist's order.

## Changed

- **You sign in through your own Spotify app.** Spotify no longer accepts new sign-ins through the shared app, and Spotsie no longer carries it. If you signed in before 0.1.4 you stay signed in until Spotify refuses the old sign-in. Spotsie offers to move you to your own app, and when the time comes onboarding opens at the setup steps. Settings shows the app as **Your Spotify app**.
- **The player bar fits any window.** As the window narrows, the controls fade out one at a time, the least used first, until only Play and Pause is left, and they come back in order as it widens. Nothing overlaps in small or tiled windows any more.

## Fixed

- **Repeat no longer flickers.** Going from Repeat all to Repeat one used to flash Repeat off for a moment. The button now goes straight to the mode you chose, even when another device is playing.

Spotsie needs Spotify Premium. Spotsie is not affiliated with Spotify.
