A small release that brings back how the cover and lyrics views felt before 0.1.1.

## Changed

- **Switching between the cover and the lyrics is instant again.** The cover no longer glides and resizes when you swap views or when the lyrics arrive. Opening or closing the queue beside the lyrics still glides the cover between its large and small places.
- **The player bar over the cover and lyrics is quick again.** It slides in and out at its earlier speed and steps aside 1.5 seconds after the pointer leaves. It still stays up while a menu is open.

Playback needs Spotify Premium. Spotsie is not affiliated with Spotify.
