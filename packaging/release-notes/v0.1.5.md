This release fixes playback getting stuck after your computer sleeps. Spotsie could keep playing the old song while every new one you picked spun and went back to it. Spotsie now notices when its connection to Spotify is gone and reconnects on its own, and the song you picked plays as soon as it is back.

## Fixed

- **Picking a song after sleep plays it.** After a sleep, or a day of sleeps, Spotsie's connection to Spotify could die without Spotsie noticing. The old song played on, and a new one showed a spinner and then fell back to the old one. Spotsie now reconnects as soon as you wake the computer or the connection drops, and picks up the song and queue you had.
- **A song you pick is never lost to a reconnect.** If a song you pick does not reach the player within a few seconds, Spotsie reconnects and plays it on the new connection. The song stays shown the whole time.
- **Another device taking over stops Spotsie.** When Spotsie lost its link to Spotify Connect, starting music on your phone could leave both playing. Spotsie now notices within about a minute, reconnects, and leaves the playing to the other device.
- **Less memory held after a dropped connection.** Spotsie no longer keeps a dead connection in memory after replacing it.

Spotsie needs Spotify Premium. Spotsie is not affiliated with Spotify.
