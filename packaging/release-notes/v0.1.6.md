This release adds a button to hide the sidebar, so you no longer have to know that the Library heading does it. It also brings local playback back after a long sleep without asking you to sign in again. Spotsie is now English only.

## New

- **A button to hide the sidebar.** It sits at the top right of the sidebar, beside Home. Clicking the Library heading and pressing Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on macOS) still work too.

## Fixed

- **Playback comes back after a long sleep without the browser.** Playing a song, Try again and Set up playback here now reconnect with the sign-in you already saved, and hold your song until it plays. The browser opens only when there is no saved sign-in.
- **Spotsie keeps trying if the network is slow to return.** A reconnect that fails for a passing reason is retried in the background, and the failure is shown only once.

## Changed

- **Top bar buttons feel calmer.** The round buttons fade on hover and show a focus ring when you reach them with the keyboard.

## Removed

- **Other interface languages.** Spotsie now always appears in English, and the Language setting is gone. Song titles and lyrics still show in their own language and script.

Spotsie needs Spotify Premium. Spotsie is not affiliated with Spotify.
