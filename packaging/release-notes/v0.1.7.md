This release lets you clear all your recent searches at once, and tidies the sidebar so a single button hides it.

## New

- **Clear all your recent searches at once.** The Search page now has a Clear recent searches link beside the Recent searches heading. The cross on each search still removes just that one.

## Changed

- **One button hides the sidebar.** The sidebar button beside Home (or Ctrl+B, Cmd+B on macOS) hides the sidebar. The Library heading is now just a heading.
- **The sidebar lines up with the top bar.** Home and the sidebar button now sit level with Back, Forward and search.
