The first Spotsie build: Spotifast 0.12.0's code under Spotsie's own name, ready for its new interface.

## New

- **Spotsie installs beside Spotifast.** It has its own app ID, command, settings, sign-in and data folders, so the two never share state or block each other from starting.
- **Update checks are off by default.** Turn them on under Settings once releases are published somewhere the app can reach.

## Removed

- **The Winamp mini player and the MilkDrop visualiser are gone,** with their skins, presets, shortcuts and settings. The equalizer stays in Settings, and the player bar visualizer stays. Building no longer needs CMake, libclang or a C++ compiler.

Built from [Spotifast 0.12.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.12.0) by Carmine Paolino.
