This release fixes slow pause and play, and songs starting over, after Spotsie has been open for a long time.

## Fixed

- **Songs carry on at the same spot after a short network drop.** When the connection to Spotify drops for a moment (a Wi-Fi network switching access points, say), the song you were playing comes back where it was once Spotsie reconnects, instead of being lost.
- **Play picks the song up where it was.** Pressing play after local playback went down no longer starts the song from the beginning.
- **Pause and play stay quick.** Pressing play while Spotsie was reconnecting in the background could leave it thinking playback on this computer was down although it worked, making pause take seconds and play take ten or more until Spotsie was restarted.

Spotsie needs Spotify Premium. Spotsie is not affiliated with Spotify.
